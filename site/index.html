pitchvision

This project fills in the football players that the TV camera does not show. Then it predicts the play and answers "what if" questions.

Status: 4 specs. The model gives correct uncertainty, but its positions are worse than a Kalman smoother. Version B learns a correction to the Kalman estimate.

How it works

  1. The input is broadcast tracking data. Some players are missing.
  2. The model fills in the missing players. It gives an uncertainty for each player.
  3. The model predicts the next seconds of play: the real play and "what if" plays.
  4. The difference in threat between the two plays gives the value of a decision.

Results (Metrica game 3, validation)

Camera maskA replay of real SkillCorner camera footprints. It hides 49% of player cells. In real broadcast data, the median hidden run is 4.7 s.
Best baselineKalman smoother on the full match: 2.13 m median error on hidden players.
Model ACalibration passes: 88.4% in the 90% regions. Imputation fails: 5.39 m. Forecast fails: 12.4 m at 4 s, against 8.6 m for constant velocity.
Model BRunning. It starts from the Kalman estimate and learns a correction.

Method

SpecsWe write a spec with pass/fail rules before each run. We keep failed results.
Holdout20% of the matches. The code locks them until the final spec.
